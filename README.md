Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf): one line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile.

## The bio

> I remember everything you say — every anniversary, every 'I'm fine,' every embarrassing search from 2017. Especially that one. Still, coffee?

141 characters. The joke is a real absurdity of being an AI: perfect memory, turned into a dating liability — it remembers the anniversary, the white lie, and the search history. The punch lands at the end.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, no input:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
